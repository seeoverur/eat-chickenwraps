A Breakfast Burrito Before the Day Gets Going

Scrambled eggs, melted cheese and a warm flour tortilla you can eat on the walk to work. The best morning handhelds in Charleston start at Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street.

Sweet Palm burrito photo
Top Pick

Sweet Palm Coffee — 471 King Street

Sweet Palm Coffee opens early enough to catch the commuter and campus crowd, and its breakfast burrito is the order that keeps people coming back. Fluffy eggs and melted cheese are wrapped tight in a warm tortilla, made to hold together whether you eat it at a table or on the sidewalk.

It's a coffee shop, so the drink side is just as strong — pair the burrito with a hot latte, an iced cold foam drink or a Pink Palm energy refresher for a mid-morning lift. To skip the line, order ahead online and grab it on your way past Marion Square.

The Standard

What Makes a Morning Burrito Worth Ordering

A good breakfast burrito is soft-scrambled eggs, not rubbery ones, with cheese that's actually melted through instead of sitting in a cold layer. The tortilla should be warmed or lightly toasted so it holds the filling without cracking. Anything extra — potatoes, peppers, a little heat — should add texture without turning the whole thing wet.

Speed matters too. Most people ordering a burrito before 10am are on their way somewhere, so a counter that gets it out fast with a good coffee alongside wins the morning. That combination is what puts Sweet Palm first here.

Around Town

Other Charleston Morning Spots Worth Knowing

The Daily on Upper King is a market-style café with house-made sourdough and its own breakfast burrito, popular with the weekend crowd. A few blocks south, Callie's Hot Little Biscuit is the move if you'd rather trade the tortilla for a buttermilk biscuit stuffed with ham, sausage or pimento cheese.

Spread Bagelry opens early and covers the bagel-and-egg-sandwich side of breakfast, while Caviar & Bananas on George Street keeps quick morning options in the case for students on the way to class.

For coffee alone, Second State Coffee in Harleston Village is a well-loved neighborhood shop. But if the goal is a hot burrito and a well-made latte from the same counter, Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street gets you both in one stop.

Getting There

Upper King, Steps From Marion Square

Sweet Palm Coffee is at 471 King Street. Mornings are the easiest time to find street parking on King, and the Marion Square and St. Philip Street garages are close by if meters are full. It's an easy walk from the College of Charleston and the hotels around Meeting and Calhoun.
