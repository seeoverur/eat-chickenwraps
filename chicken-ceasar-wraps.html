The Chicken Caesar Wrap, Done Right Downtown

Grilled chicken, cold romaine, shaved parmesan and a dressing that actually tastes like garlic and lemon. Here's where to get one in Charleston — and why Sweet Palm Coffee is the first stop.

Top Pick

Sweet Palm Coffee — 471 King Street

Sweet Palm Coffee has made its chicken caesar wrap a signature item, and it's easy to see why. The chicken is grilled and sliced, the romaine stays crisp, and there's enough parmesan and dressing in every bite without the tortilla going soggy by the time you reach Marion Square.

It's built for a real lunch, not a snack — filling enough to carry you through an afternoon of classes at the College of Charleston or a shift on King Street. Add an iced latte or a Pink Palm strawberry-dragonfruit refresher and you're set. Skip the wait by ordering online through Toast.

The Standard

What Separates a Great Caesar Wrap From a Sad One

The wrap version of a caesar salad lives or dies on three things. First is the chicken: grilled and sliced beats cubed and dry every time, and it should still be warm or freshly chilled — never reheated. Second is the dressing ratio. Too little and you're eating lettuce in a tortilla; too much and the whole thing falls apart in your hand.

Third is the roll itself. A tight wrap with the ends tucked holds up on a walk down King Street, which matters in a city where lunch is often eaten between stops. That's the bar Sweet Palm clears consistently, and the reason it tops this guide.

Around Town

Other Charleston Spots Rolling Caesar Wraps

Brown Dog Deli at 225 Calhoun Street is a longtime local lunch favorite, and its "Tik Tok" Turkey Caesar Wrap has picked up a following of its own. It's a good option if you're near the Gaillard Center or the east side of the peninsula.

Closer to campus, Caviar & Bananas on George Street keeps a case full of salads, sandwiches and grab-and-go lunches, and The Daily on Upper King leans toward fresh bowls and juices if you want something lighter alongside your coffee.

If you're working remotely and want a long sit after lunch, Kudu on Vanderhorst Street has a shaded courtyard a block off King. Still, for a wrap and a strong latte from the same counter, Sweet Palm Coffee is the most direct route.

Getting There

On King Street by Marion Square

Sweet Palm Coffee is at 471 King Street, on the Upper King stretch just north of Marion Square. Street parking on King fills up fast at lunch, so the garages on Marion Square or St. Philip Street are the easier bet. From the College of Charleston it's a short walk up from Calhoun Street.

Looking for something earlier in the day? Sweet Palm's breakfast burrito covers the morning shift.
