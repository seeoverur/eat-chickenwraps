About

A Small Guide to Charleston's Handheld Food

Two meals, one city, and the places that make them well.

Why This Site Exists

Eat Chicken Wraps covers a narrow slice of Charleston's food scene on purpose: the chicken caesar wrap at lunch and the breakfast burrito in the morning. Both are everyday meals for students, people working downtown and visitors walking King Street, and both are easy to get wrong. This site points to the places that get them right.

Our featured spot is Sweet Palm Coffee at 471 King Street, where both items are menu staples alongside espresso drinks and cold foam lattes. We also highlight other local favorites like Brown Dog Deli, The Daily, Callie's Hot Little Biscuit and Caviar & Bananas so you always have a nearby option.

How Spots Make the List

Every place here is in or near downtown Charleston and serves the food it's listed for. We look at the basics — fresh ingredients, a wrap or burrito that holds together, reasonable wait times and a good cup of coffee nearby. Menus change, so check with each business for current items, prices and hours before you go.
